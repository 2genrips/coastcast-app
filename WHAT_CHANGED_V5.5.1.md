# CastVector 5.5.1

## Community Live moved to the top bar
- Community Live now has its own chat bubble beside the Profile button.
- The bubble shows a green live indicator and current anglers-online count.
- Unread chat messages appear as a compact badge on the bubble.
- The old Community Live shortcut card was removed from Trips.
- The full Community page, channels, reports, moderation, typing, pins and chat backend are unchanged.

No Supabase SQL changes are required for this update.
