# CoastCast 2.2 Brand Guide

## Primary identity
- Emblem: compass + fish + wave + location cue.
- Wordmark: COASTCAST / FISHING FORECAST.
- Tone: premium marine utility, not cartoon fishing artwork.

## Core palette
- Deep Navy `#06182A`
- Deep Surface `#04131F`
- Ocean Blue `#0B3F6F`
- Electric Blue `#1479E8`
- Aqua `#16C7D8`
- Seafoam `#59E0C0`
- Ice `#DFF8FF`

## Asset usage
- `brand-emblem.png`: header/logo mark and branded panels.
- `brand-primary.png`: full promotional lockup / store and social material.
- `brand-watermark.png`: background only, displayed at roughly 2–4% opacity.
- `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png`: installed PWA/app icons.

## Watermark rule
Keep the watermark subtle. It should be felt before it is noticed and must never reduce forecast readability.
