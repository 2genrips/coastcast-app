# Tester feedback checklist

Ask testers to report:
- phone model and Android version
- whether install/open worked
- whether location permission made sense
- whether Home answered "should I go / when / target / bait" clearly
- weather, marine and tide loading failures
- wrong or suspicious map/tackle locations
- slow screens or freezes
- confusing Premium locks
- sign-in or server-access problems
- Community Live send/receive failures
- online count or unread badge problems
- report/block/moderation issues
- broken links to Privacy, Terms, Support or Delete Account
- any app crash
