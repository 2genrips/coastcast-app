# AnglerSignal 2.1 Brand Guide

## Identity
AnglerSignal combines a compass, coastal fish, wave, and location motif. The emblem is the primary app mark.

## Core colors
- Deep Navy: #06182A
- Deep Ocean: #04131F
- Ocean Blue: #0B3F6F
- Signal Blue: #1479E8
- Coast Aqua: #16C7D8
- Seafoam: #59E0C0
- Ice White: #DFF8FF

## Usage
- `coastcast-emblem.png`: app header, icon applications, cards, profile and map branding.
- `coastcast-primary-logo.png`: brand pages, launch/promotional graphics.
- `coastcast-watermark-ice.png`: very low-opacity app background only.
- Keep watermark opacity subtle; it should never reduce readability.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or add decorative effects to the primary mark.

## Voice
Clear, confident, coastal, practical. AnglerSignal should feel like a serious fishing utility rather than a novelty app.

Tagline: **Plan smarter. Fish better.**
