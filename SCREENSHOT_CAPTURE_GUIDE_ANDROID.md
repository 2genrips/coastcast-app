# CastVector Google Play screenshot capture guide

Google highly recommends at least four phone screenshots at 1080px or higher. Use portrait screenshots when possible.

Capture these 6 screens from the current CastVector build on your phone:

1. HOME — Quick Answer visible
   Goal: immediately show Should I go / when / target / conditions.

2. FORECAST — wind, marine and tide information visible
   Goal: demonstrate depth without looking cluttered.

3. MAP / SCOUT
   Goal: show fishing-location intelligence and saved waters.

4. TRIPS — Coast Watch or Trip Planner
   Goal: show that CastVector helps plan the whole trip, not only weather.

5. COMMUNITY LIVE
   Goal: show chat channels and anglers-online count. Avoid showing real email addresses or private information.

6. PROFILE / PREMIUM
   Goal: show CastVector Premium benefits, not your private Owner Console.

## Before taking screenshots
- Use the latest CastVector version.
- Remove personal notifications from the status bar where possible.
- Do not show personal email addresses, private waypoints or secret Owner controls.
- Use a realistic coastal destination with live data loaded.
- Keep the phone in portrait orientation.
- Do not add words like #1, Best, New, Sale, Download now or Install now.

Place the final screenshots in store/google-play-assets/screenshots/ before uploading to Play Console.
