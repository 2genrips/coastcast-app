Account, Premium, forecast, data and app-install support.

Account & Premium

If access looks wrong, sign in and use Profile → Refresh server access. Family and complimentary access are tied to the CastVector account email, not the phone.

Forecast or station issues

Refresh Live Data and review source-status badges. CastVector combines multiple public/third-party sources and will identify fallback or unavailable data rather than treating every source as live.

Installed app not updating

Close CastVector completely and reopen it after a deployment. Android may cache home-screen icons longer than app code; reinstalling the PWA can be required for an icon change.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account from Profile. If you cannot sign in, use the public deletion-request page.
