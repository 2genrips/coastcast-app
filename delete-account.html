ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your AnglerSignal account and associated data.

If you can sign in, the fastest path is inside AnglerSignal: Profile → Delete account & cloud data.

What deletion covers

After verification and completion, AnglerSignal removes the account and account-linked cloud data such as synced fishing data, community posts, family links, entitlements and push subscriptions, except information that must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or legal reasons as described in the Privacy Policy. When paid app-store subscriptions are active, account deletion does not automatically cancel store billing; cancel the subscription through the store as well.

Can't sign in? Submit a deletion request

We will log the request for ownership verification before deleting an account.
