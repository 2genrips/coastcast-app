# Screenshot capture plan

Google Play requires at least 2 screenshots; 4+ high-resolution phone screenshots are recommended.

Capture these from the CURRENT CastVector build on your Galaxy after hiding unrelated notifications:

1. Home — Quick Answer + fishing score
2. Forecast — tide/wind/surf detail
3. Map or Scout — fishing-location intelligence
4. Trips — Coast Watch / planner
5. Community Live — chat + anglers online
6. Profile — Premium membership / Family Crew

Use normal app screenshots with no fingers or phone-frame mockups.
Do not upload old CoastCast or AnglerSignal branded screenshots.
Do not show private email addresses, exact private waypoints, API keys, or Supabase admin screens.
