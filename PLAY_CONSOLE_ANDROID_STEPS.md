# Play Console steps from your Android phone

1. Open Play Console in Chrome.
2. Create the app:
   - App name: CastVector Fishing Forecast
   - Default language: English (United States)
   - App or game: App
   - Free or paid: Free (Premium is an in-app subscription)
3. Use package ID `com.castvector.fishing`.
4. Complete App content:
   - Privacy policy
   - Ads declaration
   - App access
   - Target audience
   - Content rating
   - Data safety
   - Account deletion
5. Complete Main store listing using `STORE_LISTING_EN_US.txt`.
6. Upload `app_icon_512.png` and `feature_graphic_1024x500.png`.
7. Capture and upload at least 4 current phone screenshots if possible.
8. Create subscription product `castvector_premium_monthly`.
9. Start with Internal testing for your own device.
10. Move to Closed testing when the app setup is complete.
11. Add testers by email list or Google Group.
12. Upload the signed `.aab` produced by GitHub Actions.
13. Share the closed-test opt-in link with testers.
