HOW IT WORKS

Species-adjusted score

CoastCast starts with weather, wind, waves, tide movement and daylight. Species Mode then shifts the weighting to better match how you want to fish. Your logged catches now add a personal pattern signal when CoastCast finds similar conditions.

TidesMovement and timing relative to high/low water
WindSpeed and casting direction for the selected fishing style
MarineWave height, period, swell and water temperature
WeatherRain risk, pressure and temperature
DaylightDawn and dusk windows receive extra weight
Your historyCatch Intelligence compares current conditions with your successful catches
