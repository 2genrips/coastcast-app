PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy built around your fishing data.

Effective September 4, 2026. This policy describes how AnglerSignal handles account, location, fishing, community and device data.

At a glance

AnglerSignal can be used with an account for server-verified access and cloud features. Exact fishing locations are treated as sensitive. Community sharing is controlled by the angler, and private catches or waypoints are not intentionally published unless the user chooses a public sharing option.

Data AnglerSignal may process

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifiers, account status, Premium entitlement source, Family Crew relationships and administrative access records.
  • Location data: current or destination coordinates when you grant location access or choose a fishing destination; saved waypoints may include exact coordinates.
  • Fishing data: catches, photos, species, measurements, bait, notes, trip plans, alerts, tackle information, favorite waters and fishing preferences.
  • Community data: posts, catch-card content and the location precision you deliberately select for sharing.
  • Device and notification data: push-subscription information and basic technical information needed to deliver notifications or operate the service.
  • Subscription records: store purchase identifiers, product information and entitlement status. AnglerSignal does not receive your full payment-card number from an app store.

How data is used

Data is used to operate accounts, synchronize your data when enabled, personalize forecasts and fishing recommendations, provide Premium and Family access, deliver requested alerts, display community content you choose to publish, prevent abuse, support users and maintain the service.

Third-party services

AnglerSignal uses or may use service providers and public data sources to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication/database/backend functions; weather and marine data providers such as Open-Meteo; NOAA and National Weather Service data; OpenStreetMap/Leaflet map resources; and optional places/business data providers configured by AnglerSignal. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices.

Location and fishing-spot privacy

Location can be used to provide nearby forecasts, tides, stations, fishing access and tackle information. If cloud sync is enabled, saved data that contains coordinates can be stored with your account. When sharing to Community, AnglerSignal provides location-privacy choices. Do not publish an exact spot unless you intend to make it public.

Data retention and deletion

Account-linked data is retained while needed to provide AnglerSignal and while your account is active, subject to legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal or operational retention needs. You can request deletion from inside AnglerSignal under Profile → Account & secure access, or use the public account-deletion page if you cannot sign in. When an account deletion is completed, AnglerSignal deletes or de-identifies account-associated data that is not required to be retained for a legitimate reason.

Account deletion

Your choices

  • Decline phone location permission and enter a destination manually.
  • Choose private/general/public location precision for supported sharing features.
  • Disable notifications.
  • Disable cloud auto-sync.
  • Export a local AnglerSignal backup.
  • Delete your AnglerSignal account and associated server data.

Children

AnglerSignal is a fishing-planning service and is not intentionally designed to collect personal data from children under the age required to consent to online services in their jurisdiction. Family access should be managed by an adult account holder where applicable.
